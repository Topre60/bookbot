      How Inkling works

      Dialogue, fast

      1. Add your characters with + Cast in the dock. Give each a one-letter button, like J and L.
      2. Tap J. You get an open quote. Type the line and press Enter.
      3. The line closes with the tag, punctuation fixed:
        “Did you bring it?” asks Jun.
      4. Turn on ⇄ Back and forth and Enter hands the next line to the other character. Press Enter on an empty quote to stop.
      5. Already wrote the line? Highlight it and tap a character. Highlight anything and tap “ ” to just quote it.

      Prose and screenplay

      1. Switch between Prose, Script and Poem at the top. Inkling can convert the text: tagged dialogue becomes character and dialogue blocks, and back again.
      2. In Script, Enter picks the next element for you (Character → Dialogue → Action). Lines starting with INT. or EXT. become scene headings.
      3. Script pages export as Fountain, which Final Draft, Highland and WriterSolo open.

      Typing helpers

      1. Type " and the closing quote appears too, with the cursor in between. Type the closing quote and it steps over it. Backspace on an empty pair removes both.
      2. Select text and type ", ( or [ to wrap it.
      3. Thesaurus: put the cursor in a word and tap Thesaurus for similar words, opposites, rhymes and describing words. Tap one to swap it in.
      4. ‹ Word and Word › select one word at a time; tap again to grow the selection. ⌫ Word deletes the word before the cursor.
